Outline and text button hover colors, with the other button colors

Color › Interactive, in Studio and the plugin (one shell). The owner decided where these colors go: with each button set's fill and state pickers, not under the outline hover method's chooser. This page shows that placement before and after, and asks only the questions still open.

How to read this. Every frame is a mock of the levers panel, drawn with the Studio chrome's own colors and the example brand's resolved colors (prism3, at main 2ef39ee6). Use the method menu to see each method. The dashed outline marks the group that moves. Tags such as Q3116.1 point to an open question at the end. Where a question is open, the "after" frames draw today's build and say so. The one exception is Q3116.3: the frames draw its option A, and it is still open. All new wording is DRAFT.

What is there today

  • #3335 already put the Solid fill picks in each button set's card. They sit in an "Outline & text hover" group that is seventh in the card, after Fill, Fill · inverse, Text, Text · inverse, Border and Border · inverse. In the 1280 panel that is about 1,600px below Fill.
  • The group shows only under Solid fill. Under the two washes, the card shows read-only "Overlay wash" or "Tinted wash" swatches in the same place. Under No hover fill, it shows nothing.
  • The method is one brand-wide choice (Q3335.6 A): four options in the Interactive settings card, stacked one per line since #3115. Wash opacity sits under it.
  • The label and border have hover and pressed rows already, in the card's Text and Border groups. Those colors are shared: the same Text and Border roles also color tabs, tags, pagination, segmented controls, switches and checkboxes. That is why Q3116.1 is a real choice.

What the page proposes

  • The group moves up, directly under Fill, so the filled button's hover and pressed and the outline and text buttons' hover and pressed read as one block.
  • The group is set up like every other color group in the card (owner direction, 2026-11-18): a Rest row, with Hover and Pressed as inset rows under it. Each row has its swatch, its name and token path, and an "Auto · <step>" picker with its contrast ratio, as in Fill, Text and Border. There is no separate two-picker row and no separate read-only layout.
  • The group shows under every method, always with the same title. Under Solid fill the rows show the picked fills. Under a wash the same rows show the wash values, with a line saying where the method and opacity change. Under No hover fill it shows one line. Today's separate "Overlay wash" and "Tinted wash" groups fold into it. The owner's A doesn't settle this, so it is open: Q3116.3.
  • The method chooser and Wash opacity stay in Interactive settings, because they apply to every button set. Their form is Q3116.2.
  • Nothing in the engine changes for the placement: same levers, same tokens, same writes. It is a Studio layout change only.

Open questions

Each can be answered in one line, such as "Q3116.1 A". The recommendation is listed first, as A. None of them is built, and this page picks none of them.

Q3116.1 Which parts of outline and text buttons can a brand change for hover and pressed?

Today a brand can pick the fill (under Solid fill). The label and border have hover and pressed rows too, but those colors are shared with tabs, tags, pagination, segmented controls, switches and checkboxes. So changing the outline button's hover border today also changes those.

  1. A (rec). The fill only, as built. The group holds the fill's rows: Rest, with Hover and Pressed inset. The label and border keep their own rows in Text and Border. The label still moves itself where it needs to, to stay legible on the fill you pick.
  2. B. The fill, plus a label and border color that belong to outline and text buttons only, for hover and pressed. Changing them would not touch tabs or tags. This adds new colors to every button set in every mode, so it needs engine work and new tokens.
  3. C. The fill, and the group also shows the existing Text and Border hover and pressed rows, moved in from their groups, inset under a Text and a Border row as they are today. Same colors as today. A line says which other controls they also change.

Why A: it needs nothing new, and nothing on the page changes colors you didn't expect. B is the only option where the outline button's label and border change without moving tabs and tags. It is worth building if testing shows the shared colors get in the way.

Q3116.2 What form should the method chooser take?

The chooser is brand-wide and stays in Interactive settings. Today it is four options, stacked one per line since the #3115 fix, with Wash opacity under it.

  1. A (rec). Two short choices. "Hover fill": Wash · Solid · None. Then, only under Wash, "Wash color": Neutral · Tinted. Each fits on one row at 380.
  2. B. Keep the four options as they are today.
  3. C. A list with one option per line and a one-line description under each.
  4. D. A menu (a select) with the four options, and the chosen one's description under it.

Why A: it splits the two things the four names mix: whether there is a fill, and what color the wash is. It also keeps Studio's rule of chips for two to four options (#1675), with short labels that don't wrap. It writes the same four values as today, so nothing in the engine changes. The labels are DRAFT.

Q3116.3 Does the group show under every method, with the wash groups folded into it?

The owner's A puts these colors beside Fill. It doesn't say what the card shows under the other three methods. Today the "Outline & text hover" group shows only under Solid fill. Under a wash, the card shows a separate read-only "Overlay wash" or "Tinted wash" group instead, and under No hover fill it shows nothing. The "after" frames draw A.

  1. A (rec). One group under every method, as drawn. It is always titled "Outline & text hover" and always sits directly under Fill. It holds the same rows as Fill under Solid fill and under a wash (showing the wash values), and one line under No hover fill. The "Overlay wash" and "Tinted wash" groups fold into it.
  2. B. The group under Solid fill only, as today. The "Overlay wash" and "Tinted wash" read-outs stay as they are, with their own titles, but move up beside Fill too. Under No hover fill, nothing shows.
  3. C. The two wash groups kept separate and where they are today, after Border · inverse. Only the Solid fill group moves up beside Fill.

Why A: the hover colors are in the same place, under the same title, whichever method the brand picks, and under a wash the group says where to change them. B and C keep today's split, and C leaves the wash read-outs far below Fill. All three are layout only: same levers, tokens and writes.

Q3105.1 Should the pressed contrast check also cover the two washes?

Under Solid fill, the label is checked against both the hover fill and the pressed fill. Under the neutral and tinted washes, the pressed state is still checked against the page, or not at all. One known case falls short: on one brand's high-contrast modes, a pressed label on the neutral wash measures 6.99:1 against a 7:1 floor.

  1. A (rec). As built: the check covers Solid fill only, and a follow-up issue covers the washes.
  2. B. Extend the check to every method now.

Why A: the washes are a separate change, and checking them would move values for every brand that uses the default wash.

Q3105.2 When the label is too faint on the pressed fill, what gives way?

Pressed is the next step past hover unless the brand picks one. If the label doesn't clear its floor on that step, one of the two has to move.

  1. A (rec). As built: the fill stays as picked, and the label moves along its own color until it clears. If no step clears both the fill and the page, the label stays put and a warning names the miss.
  2. B. The pressed fill steps back toward the page until the label clears.

Why A: it keeps the brand's chosen step exactly, and it is the same way links already stay legible (#1510).

Not covered here

  • Tags, segmented controls and pagination. They keep the neutral wash under every method (Q3071.4 A), so Wash opacity stays visible under Solid fill (Q3335.4 A).
  • The other button sets. Neutral, Destructive and any added set get the same group in the same place. The frames draw Primary only.
  • The style guide pages and the preview's own layout. Neither changes.
