Menu, Popover and Tooltip: wireframe

For the three proposals filed from #4001. Grey boxes to show parts, axes and members, not a visual design. Each OD marker points at an owner decision in that component's proposal; the wireframe draws the recommended option and nothing here is decided.

Motion uses stand-ins for motion.transition.enter / exit; reduced motion fades only.

0 · How the three relate

Recommended (OD-X1): no shared Figma component. Menu and Popover bind the same raised-surface roles, held equal by a test; Tooltip is on the inverse surface. In code, one floating layer (anchor, placement, collision, dismissal, top layer) serves all three, and later Select's listbox.
