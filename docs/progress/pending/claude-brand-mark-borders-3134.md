## (2026-10-28) — Brand marks: tile edges only where the fill needs one, black Apple and Facebook glyphs, a canvas grid that clears wide marks (#3134)

The owner previewed the template file and gave three directions, all decided on the issue.

- **Tile edges.** The 1px edge exists so a white tile reads as a card on a white page. `PartDef.tileEdge` gains `edgeWhen`, which narrows the stroke alone: a tile it leaves out keeps its bound corners and its crop and draws no stroke or weight. BrandMark computes the list from each file's first fill against white, under 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11's non-text floor), so a provider added later is judged the day it lands. Computed per tile: white tiles (Affirm, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, Diners Club, Discover, Google Pay, Maestro, Mastercard, PayPal, Visa) 1.00:1, edged; Klarna `#FEB4C7` 1.67:1, edged; Venmo `#008CFF` 3.39:1, Amex `#1F72CD` 4.83:1, Shop Pay `#5A31F4` 6.60:1 and the generic card `#545454` 7.57:1, no edge. The generic card has no ground: its first fill is the card body itself.
- **The gate is independent of the def.** `lint-mark-colors.ts` (e) re-reads the raw file with its own regex and its own luminance arithmetic, and holds both directions: an edge on a tile that needs none fails, and a missing one fails. The executor arms (`test-write-components.ts`, the paste twin in `test.ts`) type the four edgeless tiles by name.
- **Black glyphs.** `provider/apple` and `provider/facebook` were drawn white and vanished on the canvas. They are `#000000` now, records updated (`mono-black`; the source line says why). A consumer tints them as needed, per the owner.
