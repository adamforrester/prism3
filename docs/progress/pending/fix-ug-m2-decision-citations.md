## (2026-11-11) — Decision citations are held back from the component docs (UG-M2, part of #3552)

The maintainer register (`MAINTAINER_REGISTER`, `packages/engine/component-doc-sections.ts`) holds back a part note that cites an issue, a doc path or a section sign, or carries a code span. It didn't recognize an owner-decision id or a QA date. So "(Q414 A)", "(Q257)" and "owner QA 2026-10-19" reached reader captions on the usage panels and the docs pages. The 2026-11-11 usage-guide scan counted 9 such captions on 4 panels (class N2).

**The fix:** the register adds `\bQ\d+\b` and `owner QA <year>`. Under the existing rule (Q406 follow-up 4 A), a held-back note becomes a gap: the part is listed by name with no caption, and `docGaps` records it. That's 14 notes across all defs, more than the scan's 9, because Tabs' label reserve, Segmented control's label and Pagination.Item's three glyphs aren't on a lead panel but are on their docs pages. Rewriting them instead is the owner's call, Q-UG1 on #3552. If the owner picks B or C there, this rebases onto that lane. `components.ai.json` doesn't move, because it carries the def's notes as they are.

**The gate:** `lint-component-docs.ts` restates the register as `MAINTAINER_MARK`, spelled independently (`\(Q\d`, `Q\d+ [A-Za-z]\)`, `owner QA \d`), and its detector samples now include three citation shapes plus one near miss ("A Quiet 2px gap."). `test-doc-panel.ts`'s `legend/pagination` is typed by hand, so its four Q414 lines now read with no caption. `anatomy/held back` imports the engine's register and can't fail on this change (`docs/34` shape 1), so it isn't counted as a gate here. Mutations: the new alternatives dropped from the engine's register → `lint-component-docs` fails `MAINTAINER CAPTION (model)` once for each of the 14 parts, naming `pagination` `previous` among them, and `legend/pagination` fails. The new alternatives dropped from the gate's `MAINTAINER_MARK` → `ANATOMY DETECTOR` fails for each of the three citation samples, and `CAPTION DROPPED (model)` and `ANATOMY LINE (page)` fail for each part.
