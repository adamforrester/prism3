## (2026-10-25) — test:chrome: the #2321 Shift+Tab probe waits for the mode control's fit (#3005)

Since IA v2 phase 1 (#2995), the `chrome` job failed one check at random, always on the figma host and at a different viewport each run: `#2321 figma <theme> <width> on <page>: the mode control is live (… Shift+Tab from Inspect lands in it false …)`. No control was ever disabled. Only the Shift+Tab probe missed.

**Root cause: a race in the test, not an unreachable control.** A focus and fit timeline, logged in the page while the section looped, showed the same order on every failure. Focus started on Inspect, so the issue's lead was not the cause: `isVisible()` had picked the right start. Shift+Tab then landed on a mode radio. About 1.5 ms later the control's `fit()` switched to the select (`data-fit="select"`, `data-row="own"`) and hid the radios, and Chromium dropped focus to the body. `fit()` runs in the frame after the header's ResizeObserver reports, a deferral #2383 made on purpose to avoid the "ResizeObserver loop" error. So for a frame after a place change, or after the narrow Preview toggle shows a header that measured 0 while hidden, the control can still show radios that do not fit. #2995 put Contrast into the plugin's preview header, which now settles on the select at 1280 and at 380 on the plugin. That gives the stale radios a frame in which to be wrong.

**The fix.** `pmShiftTab` waits for that fit before its walk. It makes a ResizeObserver on the preview header, which is notified after the control's own (observers are notified in the order they were created), and waits one frame after that, by which point the fit the control asked for has run. A header that never reports rejects within 5 s and stops the case by name. The assertion is unchanged, and the diff is that one function.

**Proof.** The section was extracted and looped, five copies in parallel for load. Before the fix, 12 of 15 runs failed, with 22 failures between them, all on figma at 380. After it, 25 runs passed with no failure (7,800 checks). Three product mutations, each after a `wip:` commit: with the radios and the select taken out of the Tab order, all 88 live cases fail, each on "Shift+Tab from Inspect lands in it false". With the control held on Depth & motion as well as Palettes, exactly the eight `on depth` cases fail. With it held everywhere, every case stops by name at choosing a mode.

**Filed, not fixed here (#3013):** when the fit switches between the radios and the select under focus, the focused control is hidden and focus falls to the body. A keyboard user on a mode radio who narrows the plugin window loses their place. It is the same mechanism the probe met, reached by a resize, and where focus should go instead is the owner's call.
