## (2026-10-26) — CI runs the chrome suite in three shards, and a gate holds every section to exactly one (#2898)

After #2883 split the gates into parallel jobs, `chrome` was the whole critical path: its suite step took 1,436 s (about 24 minutes) on main, against 12 minutes or less for every other job. It could not be split, because `apps/studio/test-chrome.mjs` is one top-level script of about 15,600 lines with no way to pick a section.

**How the suite splits without changing an assertion.** A one-off transform (TypeScript's parser over the file, not committed) wrapped each section's top-level statements in `if (runs('<id>'))`. The ids are the section numbers, made unique where two sections shared one (`29` and `29-pinned`, `47` and `47-menu`; `18-readonly`, `18-s4d` and `18-s4f` for the three banners inside 18). A declaration that any other section reads stays at the top level, outside every guard. The transform found 202 of them, computed to a fixed point, and all are free of side effects except two `esbuild` bundles of engine source, which make no assertion. Inside a guard, nothing is indented, so the diff is the guard lines and blame still points at the code. Only five things carry state across sections: the check-box counts, the hooks seen, the exemptions, the lowest readings and the assertion counters. They are the end-of-run checks' inputs. A shard writes them to its record, and `test-chrome.mjs --merge` runs those checks once over every shard. The whole suite still runs them itself.

**The shards.** `CHROME_SHARD=<n>` is set in the step's `env:`, not as an argument, so the command is the one `verify.ts` runs whole, and `lint-doc-gates`' argv arm stays exact. `chrome-shards.mjs` gives each of three shards about a third of one whole local run's 1,969 s. The split was greedy, longest section first. Three shards, not four: the `plugin` job (about 12 minutes) is the next longest, so a fourth shard would not move the critical path.

**The gate, `test:chrome-shards`.** Its expected list is the unsharded suite's own: `test-chrome.mjs --list` reaches every guard and runs none. Its actual list is what each record says ran. It never reads the shard map. It fails by name on a section in no shard, a section in two, a section the suite does not list, an assertion outside every section, or a shard that ran the end-of-run checks. A shard that crashed or never reported leaves its sections in no shard. Locally it reads the whole run's record (`all.json`).

**Proof that every assertion still runs exactly once.** Main's suite was run whole, unchanged except for one line that logs each label: 39,693 assertions, all passing, in 1,969 s. Then the three shards and the merge were run from this branch: 39,693 assertions, all passing, in 629 s, 582 s and 653 s. `compare-chrome-assertions.mjs` compares the two sides as multisets. Many labels carry a reading of their own run, such as the Activity bar's clock, a server port or the frames a glide took. So an assertion's name is its label with every run of digits masked. The diff of names is empty, and the sorted name lists are byte-identical. The exact labels have 36,513 distinct values on each side, and 126 of them differ in count. Every one of those 126 is a reading. For scale, the same comparison between two whole runs, main's baseline and this branch's unsharded run inside `npm run verify`, gives 136 exact labels that differ and 0 names.

**Mutations, each failing by name**, each on a `wip:` commit, with the mutated shard re-run for real: `47-menu` dropped from every shard gave `section "47-menu" ran in no shard`. `47-menu` in shards 1 and 3 gave `section "47-menu" ran in 2 runs (shard 1, shard 3)`. Before either of those, the gate caught a real miss: the first balanced map left out section `27`, the focus-ring sweep, because its header has a computed title the timing script could not read. The gate's first run reported `section "27" ran in no shard`.

Also checked, as the issue asked: smoke flake #2629 is not worse since #2883 started smoke earlier. Of the 317 CI runs from 2026-10-21, 5 failed in the smoke step, and all 5 were real failures on PR branches (a missing hook, a place not offered). None showed #2629's stall signature ("rendered no frame", "could not pause").
