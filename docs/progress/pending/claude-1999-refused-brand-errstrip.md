## (2026-11-03) — The refused-saved-brand notice is the chrome's error strip (#1999)

**What it is.** Owner decision Q1999.1 A. When the studio refuses a saved brand at boot (#1989), its notice at the top of the start window's body is now the chrome's error strip, `.p3-errstrip`, the pattern the owner approved for a refused restore (G18 A, N-2 A, S13.1). It has the warning glyph and its three lines in danger ink in `.p3-errstrip-text`, then Export saved brand and Clear saved brand as `.p3-btn` in a `.p3-confirm-row` under them, in both themes. Until now it was #1997's legacy error card and buttons (`.errbar`, `.barbtn`), pinned light, because that fix's freeze allowed no stylesheet change. The copy, the `refused-brand*` hooks that smoke §9 drives, and the behavior are unchanged: the notice goes once a brand loads, Export keeps the override, and Clear removes `prism3:brandInput`.

**The one rule.** At the narrow tier, `chrome.css` caps the error strip at two bar heights and scrolls it (#2105), so a refused restore's long line leaves the plugin's levers pane its room. Inside the start window, that cap would only scroll the notice's two buttons out of sight, in a body that already scrolls. One rule, `.p3-frame[data-w="narrow"] .p3-start .p3-errstrip`, lifts it there and nowhere else. #2105's cap on the plugin's error line is untouched, and section 38 still holds it. No new color, edge or type.

**The test.** A new case in `test:chrome` section 28, on the web (the plugin never sets a refused brand), in both themes at 1280 and 380. It seeds the brand as smoke §9 does and checks the notice is the error strip, first in the start window's body, with the glyph, three lines and the two buttons in a confirm row. It checks the strip does not scroll and holds both buttons, and runs the chrome probe on that state, so the controls are classified (`CONTROL_KINDS`) and the text, glyph and edges are held to their floors. Mutations, each failing by name: removing the narrow rule fails `#1999 web light 380: the notice is not cut off: it does not scroll, and both buttons sit inside it` (the strip measured 17px, its buttons under it); restoring the legacy card fails the strip arm and `every chrome control is classified — unclassified control button[refused-brand-export]`.
