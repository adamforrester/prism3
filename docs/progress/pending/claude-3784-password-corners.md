## (2026-11-15) — the Password field squares its corner under Control shape Boxed and Hairline (#3784)

**STATUS: PR open, not merged.** ENGINE {{ENGINE_VERSION}} (minor, `packages/engine/changes/claude-3784-password-corners.md`: the Password field's plans move under two Control shape values), CONTRACT stands. Owner-approved fix, found during #3785 and filed rather than fixed there because that PR was behavior-neutral.

**The defect.** Boxed and Hairline square each field's box corner through the refs a def lists in `levers.controlShape.squares`. Before #3785 that list was the `FIELD_CORNER_REFS` table, and `password-field` was never in it. The Password field is built by the same `makeTextField` factory as TextField, binds the same `radius.sm` corner and already took Field style, so under Boxed or Hairline with Outline fields it kept a 4px corner beside siblings at 0px or 1px. The owner direction from #2361 is "1px corners everywhere". The fix is the one entry its siblings carry: `controlShape: { squares: ['radius.sm'] }`.

**What moves, measured.** A plan diff over every def × Control shape (4) × Field style (2), on six brand inputs (the minimal brand and the five example briefs) and both materializer chains (the engine's `brandMaterializer` and the plugin's `materializerFor`): only `password-field` differs, and only under Boxed × Outline and Hairline × Outline. Inside those, the only changed leaves are the four corner bindings of its control box, on all 84 members: `radius/sm` → `radius/none` (Boxed) or `radius/hairline` (Hairline). Under Underline the corner was already `radius.none`, set by `applyFieldStyle` before Control shape runs, so nothing changes there. `regen --check` moves no committed artifact, and the three uncommitted catalog exports are byte-identical, because the brand-neutral build draws Rounded and `levers` is not emitted.

**The test.** The #2361 corner block in `test.ts` names each field it asserts, so password-field joins its Hairline, Rounded, Pill and Boxed lines, and the #3698 R5 reach table lists it under `controlShape`. Removing the entry again fails `#2361 hairline: password-field binds radius/hairline` and `#2361 boxed: password-field binds radius/none` by name.
