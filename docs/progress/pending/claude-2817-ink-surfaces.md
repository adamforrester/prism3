## (2026-10-19) — Contrast: primary and link inks clear 4.5:1 on every surface a component places them on (#2817, Q415 A, narrowed by Q419 A)

**What was measured.** The outline / text primary ink was gated on the page (`background.primary`) and the link on the page floor, yet components draw both on more: Dialog's panel (`foreground.primary`, the footer's Button group) and, once Banner (#2786) lands, a status tint (`foreground.<status>-subtle`, its actions). On `main` the primary ink measured 3.76:1 on `foreground.primary` and 3.30:1 on a tint (aurora light), and the regression fixture's link 4.02:1 on the success tint. High contrast already cleared.

**The surface set (Q419 A).** The first cut held the inks on all 11 grounds, the literal reading of Q415's "`foreground.*`". Review found no component places them on `background.secondary` / `.tertiary` or `foreground.secondary` / `.tertiary`, and `foreground.tertiary` alone drove most of the cost (ΔE 8–15 in both modes of nearly every brand). The owner narrowed the contract to the seven surfaces components place the inks on: `background.primary`, `foreground.primary` and the five `foreground.<status>-subtle` tints. This also resolves Banner's Q398. A consumer who puts these inks on another ground gets no promise; a def that does adds the ground.

**The fix, in `modes.ts`.** No new rule: `pickBrand`'s existing `alsoClear` (the semantic-ink and `border.tertiary` mechanism) now carries the seven grounds (`INK_SURFACES`) for the primary text pick and the page link pick. The anchor stays where it clears them all; otherwise the nearest step on the same ramp that does. `pickBrand` / `chromatic` gain a trailing `alsoMin`, because the page keeps the mode's own bar (7:1 in HC) while the other grounds are held to the 4.5:1 body floor the owner set. The inverse band's twins are not placed on these grounds and do not move.

**What moves.** Dark mode is unchanged except aurora's primary ink (accent 450 → 400). In light mode the inks move one step: aurora's primary 500 → 600 and link 550 → 600, the regression fixture 550 → 600, the second client fixture 500 → 550. harbor and prism3 do not move. The only HC value that moves is aurora's hc-light 3:1 icon link (550 → 600). The hover and pressed inks (and the link's hover, pressed and visited) walk from the new rest. `interactive.primary.icon.*` / `.border.*` share the ink's candidate (#1471, #576), and `icon.link.*` runs through the same link derivation. `nb-regression` compares ramps and band contracts, not these roles, so its report does not change.

**The gate, `test.ts` (10g-iv).** Five arms, measured from the resolved hexes against the seven grounds written there as literals, never `INK_SURFACES`:
- **Coverage, from the defs.** It walks every def's own fill on a ground over the defs it nests (fixed nests, slot offers, a host's `nestItemPrefers`) and finds where a primary or link ink lands: today `foreground.primary`, through Dialog. Any such ground missing from the literals fails by name. So does a literal no component places the inks on, other than the page and the five tints pending Banner (`PENDING_2817`). A pending tint missing from the literals fails too, so the list cannot narrow below the seven. When Banner lands, its tints move out of pending, and an arm says so by name.
- **Per cell.** One `ok` per brand × mode × ink × surface, so a miss names all four.
- **Followers.** The icon and border followers must equal the text ink's hex, state for state. The icon link must clear its brand's icon bar on the same grounds.
- **Same ramp.** The hue holds; only the depth moves.
- **Just enough.** In light and dark, where the primary rest ink or the link base left its anchor, the step one notch back misses somewhere.

**Mutations, each failing by name:** dropping all five tints from `INK_SURFACES` (the per-cell arm); restoring the 11 grounds (the "just enough" arm); pointing Dialog's panel at `foreground.secondary` (coverage: not in the list); adding `foreground.tertiary` to the gate's literals (coverage: no component places them there); and dropping one tint from the gate's literals (coverage: pending but not measured). Two subject mutations are true no-ops and are not counted as tests: dropping `foreground.primary`, or any single tint, from `INK_SURFACES` leaves every pick unchanged, because the remaining tints are as strict in every cell that moved.
