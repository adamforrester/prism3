## (2026-10-18) — Contrast: primary and link inks clear 4.5:1 on every surface a component places them on (#2817, Q415 A)

**What was measured.** The outline / text primary ink was gated on the page (`background.primary`) and the link on the page floor, yet both are drawn on more: the page tiers, a raised panel (`foreground.*`, Dialog's footer) and a status tint (`foreground.<status>-subtle`, Banner's actions). Over 8 inks × 11 surfaces × every emitted mode of the five committed brands, 130 of 1,760 cells were under 4.5:1 on `main`. Every miss was in light or dark; high contrast already cleared. The worst ground was `foreground.tertiary` in every brand, then `background.tertiary` / `foreground.secondary` and the tints. The lows: the primary ink at 2.90:1 on `foreground.tertiary` and 3.30:1 on a tint (aurora light), the link at 3.55–3.78:1 on `foreground.tertiary` and 4.02:1 on a tint.

**The fix, in `modes.ts`.** No new rule: `pickBrand`'s existing `alsoClear` (the semantic-ink and `border.tertiary` mechanism) now carries the eleven grounds (`INK_SURFACES`) for the primary text pick and the page link pick. The anchor stays where it clears them all; otherwise the nearest step on the same ramp that does. `pickBrand` / `chromatic` gain a trailing `alsoMin`, because the page keeps the mode's own bar (7:1 in HC) while the other grounds are held to the 4.5:1 body floor the owner set. Held to 7:1, every HC mode would have moved on the tints (5.65–6.69:1 today). The inverse band's twins are not placed on these grounds and do not move.

**What moves, and why the icons move with it.** The hover and pressed inks (and the link's hover, pressed and visited) walk from the new rest, so the whole ladder steps with it. `interactive.primary.icon.*` and `interactive.primary.border.*` share the ink's candidate by design (#1471, #576). `icon.link.*` runs through the same link derivation, so a link's glyph keeps its text's colour; a brand with `iconContrast: '3:1'` holds its icon link to 3:1 on the same grounds. Per brand, the rest ink moved one to three rungs: about ΔE00 4.6 for harbor light, 8–11 for the other light and dark cells, 15.4 for aurora light (accent 500 → 650). prism3 light did not move. The regression fixture's brand red leaves the rest ink and link in light mode (550 → 650). `nb-regression` compares ramps and band contracts, not these roles, so its report does not change.

**The gate, `test.ts` (10g-iv).** It measures each ink × surface cell from the resolved hexes, one `ok` per cell, so a miss names brand, mode, ink and surface. The inks and grounds are literals there, never `INK_SURFACES`. Two more arms: the inks stay on their own ramp (the hue holds), and the primary rest ink is "just enough" (in light and dark, one notch back toward the anchor misses somewhere). Mutations: dropping `foreground.tertiary` from `INK_SURFACES` fails 44 per-cell asserts by name (and #1895 and #352 with them); raising the surface bar to 5.3:1 (so the ink overshoots) fails the "just enough" arm by name, per brand and mode.

**Existing expectations that moved, each because the ink did.**
- **The legacy Figma fixture.** Its link rows in light and dark gain `default` and `focused` (550 → 650, 450 → 350), and `hover` and `visited` step one rung further. HC is unchanged. This is where the regression fixture moves, not `nb-regression`.
- **#1895.** The rebased-action link anchors where the fill anchors, then steps one notch to clear the surfaces: light `deep.650`, dark `pale.350`. The by-name mutation in its comment still separates the anchors.
- **#1614 / #1646.** The solid-tint example's primary hover wash goes from 10 to 20. The deeper hover ink clears the standard opacity, so the down-step no longer engages on that page. The two inverse rows still exercise the guard.
- **#352.** Aurora's 3:1 icon link now holds 3:1 on every surface, so it is measured where it binds: 3.62:1 on `foreground.tertiary`.
- **L-04.** The ΔE ≥ 6 link ladder is now asserted on the four shipping brands only. The base sits nearer the ramp end on three synthetic stress fixtures (a neutral ramp where 900 → 950 is ΔE 2.6), so those are held to distinct + floor. That is the promise `modes.ts` already states for a stress fixture.
