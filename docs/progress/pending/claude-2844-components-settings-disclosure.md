## (2026-10-20) — Components page: a family's settings collapse behind a line under its sets (#2844, Q439 A)

The owner chose option A on #2844 with one change: the disclosure goes below the family's set checkboxes, not above them. #2838 had opened the Button options in the left rail at all times; they took too much room.

- **Where they sit.** In the family's list item: the row, then its sets when open, then one disclosure line ("Button options"), then the settings block. The line is outside the family's drawer, so the chevron still opens the sets and the line opens the settings; the two are independent. Opening the sets never opens the settings.
- **Closed on open.** The block stays mounted and `hidden` while the line is closed, so `aria-controls` always names a real element and the controls keep their subscriptions and their state.
- **The wireframe's three smaller calls, as built.** The line shows on every row that has settings, not only the focused one (`settingsOf` no longer asks which family the preview shows). Opening it also focuses its family, so the preview shows what the settings change; closing it leaves the focus alone. The open state is session state (`optsOpen` in `state/components-page.ts`), like open families: it outlasts a visit to another page and a redraw at the tier flip. Reused: the fold's chevron and its rotate-on-expanded rule, the page state's open-set pattern and the list's key-based focus restore (`data-key`), so a pressed line keeps its focus across the redraw it causes.
- **A trap for whoever re-verifies.** Focusing another family no longer takes the Button options away, as #2815's step 1 arm asserted: the settings are the family's, not the preview's. That arm is replaced by the #2844 arm. Tests that read the Button options now open the line first (`openButtonOptions` in `test-chrome.mjs`, `gotoComponents` in `test-smoke.mjs`).
