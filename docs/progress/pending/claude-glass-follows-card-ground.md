## (2026-10-27) — Card glass: a glass fill in the card ground's color, beside the white glass (#3073)

Owner decision on #3073 (2026-10-27), which supersedes the issue body's "replace the white glass": keep `color.glass.surface` as it is, and add a card glass. Measured on the reference brand's wishlist card, the white glass over the bottom of a card's photo rendered lighter than the photo's own background and than neighboring cards with glass off. The tint was the problem, not the blur.

- **`color.glass.card`** is the card ground's palette color at the glass alpha, per brand × mode: light `neutral.050` (harbor `neutral.100`), dark `neutral.900`, and in high contrast the card is pure white/black, so it aliases `white-alpha.90` / `black-alpha.90`, the same as the white glass. Same alpha as `glass.surface` in every mode, so the two differ only in hue.
- **The alpha lives in the value**, as for the white glass (Figma forces a bound paint opaque). So the engine mints an alpha step of the card's color, `<palette>-<step>-alpha.<n>`, the shape of `white-alpha.70`, through the same `alphaLeaf`. Only the steps some mode reads are minted (two per brand today), not a ramp per color. `cardGlassFamily` in `modes.ts` names the family, and `tree.ts` mints the leaf through it, so the alias always resolves.
- **It follows the card through an override.** `foreground.primary` is not a refused ground, so an override can move it; the override layer re-puts the card glass when it does, and refuses an override aimed at `glass.card` itself.
- **No engine component binds glass today**, so no def changed. The reference brand's hand-built wishlist card rebinds in the master after merge.
- **Gates** (`test.ts`, "card glass <brand>: …"): the card glass's opaque color equals `foreground.primary`'s own resolved value in every mode; its alpha is the spelled 70/90; `text.primary` on it over every gray 0–255 is ≥ 4.5:1, the white glass's floor. A Figma arm checks `color/glass/card` aliases an alpha primitive of `color/foreground/primary`'s color in every color mode. The white glass's existing arm still spells its steps, so moving `glass.surface` fails that arm by name. `check-consumability.mjs` checks the card glass reaches CSS as a reference to an alpha primitive.
- **Contrast against the role's own contract.** Worst case over a gray ground, card glass reads 6.2:1 in dark (a white photo pixel under the band), 6.9–7.8:1 in light, 16.8–17.6:1 in high contrast. That clears the 4.5:1 floor the glass gate uses but not `text.primary`'s own 7:1 role contract in dark (the white glass clears it at 7.9–8.0:1). Held as an owner question on the PR, not decided here.
- **Traps for whoever re-verifies:** mutate `modes.ts` and run `regen.ts`, because the gate reads the committed emission. The wireframe greyscale arm and the frozen-fixture name arm each gained an exact allowance for the minted `neutral-<step>-alpha` steps.
- Contract 14.13.0 (MINOR: `color.glass.card` and `core.palette.neutral-900-alpha.70` added; `neutral-050-alpha.70` is brand-dependent).
