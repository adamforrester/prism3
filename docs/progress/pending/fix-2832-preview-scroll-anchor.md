## (2026-10-19) — The edit reveal anchors to the token's own card (#2832)

Editing a color on Surfaces & fills (for example `foreground.success-subtle`) pulled the preview back to the top of its section on every change. The cause was `revealSection`: it left the preview alone only when the section was wholly in view or filled the view, so a section scrolled partly past (its end on screen, as with the Subtle cards) was scrolled to its top on every edit.

- **Fix, `revealEdit` in `preview/follow-edit.ts`.** An edit on a lever that is one token's row now notes that token; the reveal targets that token's own card in the paired section, and does nothing when the card is already in view. Levers that are not a single token's row keep the section as the target, with the old wholly-visible and fills-the-view exceptions. A card out of view comes in a little under the body's top: at the section's top when the section is wholly off screen and the card still fits under it (so the earlier QA-B9 landings are unchanged), else at the card's own top.
- **Only a different lever moves the preview.** A further edit on the same lever, with the preview left where the last one put it, does nothing. If the viewer scrolled away in between, the next edit reveals again, which the existing Type checks hold (they scroll the preview home between edits on the same lever).
- **The plugin runs the same code.** `revealEdit` and the notes live in the studio's `preview/` and `shell/frame.ts`, and the plugin UI bundles that source, so the one fix covers both hosts.
- **Test:** a new `test:chrome` case (section 21b). It puts the Subtle success card mid-view with the Foreground section's top scrolled past, edits it three times and asserts the scroll position never moves and the card stays in view; then edits another lever whose card is off screen and asserts the preview moves to show it. Mutation: the reveal back on the section's top fails `#2832: editing a Subtle foreground fill with its card in view does not move the preview` and `#2832: a second value change on the same lever leaves the preview where it was`.
