## (2026-10-27) — Brand glass roles: the `glassRoles` lever, and the reference brand's card glass (#3073)

The owner's decisions on #3073 (Q806, the glass taxonomy; Q807 A; the 70% correction of 2026-10-27) replace the closed #3074, which gave every brand a fixed `color.glass.card`. Prism3 ships one glass role, `color.glass.surface` (white or black glass), unchanged. A brand adds its own through a lever.

- **`glassRoles`** (Color, an object lever): `{ <name>: { role | palette, alpha } }` adds `color.glass.<name>`. `role` reads a semantic role's palette color per mode (`foreground.primary`, the card ground); `palette` names one color (`neutral.050`, `white`, `black`). The alpha is a whole percent, 1–99, the same in every mode.
- **The alpha lives in the value.** A bound Figma paint with paint opacity renders opaque (the 10/27 finding on the issue), so the role aliases a palette alpha step: `tree.ts` mints `<palette>-<step>-alpha.<n>` through `alphaLeaf`, only for the steps a mode reads, and on white or black it reuses `white-alpha` / `black-alpha` (an alpha off that ramp's steps is refused). `glassAlphaFamily` in `modes.ts` names the family, and the tree mints through it, so the alias always resolves.
- **Put after the override layer**, so a role source reads its role's final step: an override of `foreground.primary` carries to the card glass. An override aimed at a brand glass role is refused, naming its source. `color-order.ts` opens `glass.0` so a brand role lands after `surface`.
- **Validated:** `surface` and non-kebab names are refused, as are both or neither sources, an alpha off 1–99, a source in the glass group, a translucent or missing role, and an unknown palette step.
- **The reference brand adds `card`** (fixture and redesign seed): light `neutral-050-alpha.70`, dark `neutral-900-alpha.70`, high contrast `white-alpha.70` / `black-alpha.70`. Apply Theme writes `color/glass/card` in the color collection, plus two hidden primitives.
- **Brand-dependent, not guaranteed:** `color.glass.card` and the two minted steps join `brandDependent`; `CONTRACT_VERSION` stays 14.12.0 (`token-contract --check` level `none`). `docs/30` records it as a decision.
- **Gates** (`test.ts`, "#3073 …"): `glass.surface` is value-identical in every brand (spelled rgba per mode); each added role's opaque color equals its source's per mode and its alpha the declared one; brands without the lever emit no extra glass role or alpha family; the guaranteed contract's glass group is `surface` alone. A Figma arm reads every color mode file. Two older arms exempt the brand's own glass by a spelled per-brand list (`BRAND_ADDED_GLASS`): the shared role-set arm and the inverse register.
- **Studio:** Surfaces & fills gains a Glass section: the white glass read-only, then each added fill's name, color, opacity and a remove button, and Add glass fill. Every string is a DRAFT for the owner.
- **Trap:** the gates read the committed emission, so mutate the input and run `regen.ts` before `test.ts`.
