## (2026-11-03) — The contrast floor gets its own sub-heading and says what it checks (#3366)

**What it is.** Owner decisions Q3366.1–.6, all A. In Color › Surfaces & fills, the contrast floor (`surfaces.floorStep`) leaves the "Default" tier list for its own "Contrast floor" sub-heading, between Default and Inverse. Its row reads "Measured on", with "A setting, not a token" where a tier's token sits. Under the row, always shown, are three things. First, a line that names how many colors the floor checks. Second, the six groups those colors fall in, each with a count and a sample swatch: Links, Brand and status text and icons, Secondary and tertiary text and placeholder, Disabled text and icon, Bold fills, and Button fills. Third, a line naming what it does not check: field borders, button borders and the focus ring. Inspect › Contrast › Health gains a pointer under its mode list. It names the floor in Light and Dark, and "Change the contrast floor" closes Inspect, opens Surfaces & fills (on the Settings pane at the narrow tier) and puts focus on the floor's step button. Health now says "minimum" for a pair's own ratio ("All 884 pairs at or above their minimum."), so on that panel "floor" names only the setting. The bar's verdict line is not Health and keeps "floor".

**Where the list comes from.** It is read at draw time, so the count and the groups can't drift from the engine: `floorGroups` (`state/fills-input.ts`) takes every role in the previewed mode whose `against` is the floor. That floor is the one the engine measures `foreground.brand` on, which is how the page already read it for the Auto label. The roles are grouped by name. A governed role that no group takes is not hidden: it is counted in the total and listed as "Other", a fallback that no example brand reaches.

**The diagnosis that made it small.** The engine already records the floor on each governed role: its `against` is the floor's palette path (`neutral.050`). Other roles' `against` names a role (`background.secondary`), so nothing in the engine had to change. Across the three example brands, every mode gives the same 47 roles. The floor's info button and its sentence (QA-B6) are gone, because the line under the row now says it, always. The step button keeps the accessible name the owner chose for it on #2193 ("Contrast floor, Light: Auto · …"), so the row's new visible label did not move it.

**The gate.** `test-fills-input.ts` §12 holds `floorGroups` against a literal role list typed from the issue's measured table, group by group, in every mode of prism3, aurora and harbor (12 brand modes, with a floor on that count). The same section holds the "not checked" line to the engine: no field border, button border or focus ring is measured on the floor. Mutations, each failing by name: dropping `disabled` from `FLOOR_GROUPS` fails `… the "disabled" group (Disabled text and icon) holds exactly its 2 roles` and the total, in all 12 modes. Measuring `field.border.rest` on the floor in the engine fails `… the "not checked" line holds …` and the total. `test:chrome` reads the block as rendered on both hosts, holds it to the DRAFT copy and prism3's groups as literals, and drives the Health pointer from Type at 1280 and 380 on both hosts.

**Trap found while building it.** The group swatches first set `background` inline without `data-content`, which the chrome's runtime-inline-value check refuses outside brand content. They now carry `data-content`, like a fill row's swatch, and keep their color under forced colors.
