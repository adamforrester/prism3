The semantic duration scale gains three long steps past `slower`: `motion.duration.long`, `.longer` and `.longest` (900, 1100 and 1300ms at the standard tempo, scaled by tempo), with reduced-motion twins at 0ms. Contract 15.2.0, an additive MINOR. A second input, `motionPersonality.brandOnly`, emits a brand's one-off springs, distance-scaled durations and gesture physics under `motion.brand.*` as custom-typed leaves, outside the guaranteed names. No example brand sets either input, so no existing value moves. The step names, the preset names and the brand-only fields are DRAFT for the owner.
