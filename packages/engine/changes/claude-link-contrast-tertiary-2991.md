The contrast floor is stated as the one text promise (#2991, Q689 A). Each floor-gated ink in `.ai.json` (the secondary, tertiary, status and link inks, text and icon, and their inverse twins) gains an `avoid_when` sentence naming the surfaces deeper than its floor, which carry no contrast promise by design, and the lever that moves the floor (`floorStep`, or `inverseSecondary` on the band). The surfaces are computed per brand from the resolved values. No token value or name moves.
