Two new components, ToggleButton and ToggleIconButton (#4112, owner OD-T1 to OD-T5 A): a Button and an IconButton that stay pressed, rendered as a native button with `aria-pressed`. Both are built from Button's and IconButton's own paint and spacing, unselected as Button.Neutral and IconButton.Neutral, and take Tag's selected look: a tint and a 2px outline drawn inside the box, the label weight constant, no check mark. Two appearances only, outline and text on ToggleButton and outline and ghost on ToggleIconButton (IconButton's word), and no group component. A text toggle's label is plain, with no link underline. Button's six brand settings (minimum width, icon placement, content size, label weight, text hover, outline border) reach ToggleButton as they reach Button, so its medium floor is 104px at the default multiplier; the outline border setting reaches ToggleIconButton, as it reaches IconButton. Figma sets of 120 and 60 members on a new Toggle button page. Button's, IconButton's and every other existing set are unchanged; the relations of Button, Checkbox.Row, Switch.Row, Tag and Segmented control now name ToggleButton where they named a planned toggle button.
